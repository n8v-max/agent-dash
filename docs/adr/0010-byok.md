# ADR-0010: Bring-your-own-key splits Total spend, and is deferred

Date: 2026-09-09
Status: **Proposed** — recorded, not adopted. Nothing in the repository changes on account of it.

Proposed in [ticket 57](../../.scratch/agent-dash/issues/57-byok-adr.md), which asks for the
decision and forbids the code. **Extends [ADR-0005](0005-session-cost-is-attributed-not-derived.md)
rather than reversing it**, and adds a clause to the Hidden rule that
[ADR-0008](0008-a-child-session-rolls-up-into-its-root.md) last amended. It amends neither
`CONTEXT.md` nor `spec.md`; the amendments acceptance would require are listed under
*Consequences*, so that accepting it is a bounded piece of work rather than a discovery.

## Context

**Bring-your-own-key is a shipped deployment mode, not a hypothesis.** The customer registers their
own vendor API key and the platform routes their sessions through it. The tokens are then bought by
the customer, from the vendor, on the customer's account, at the moment they are spent. The platform
bills what it still owns: seats, and machine time. OpenRouter ships BYOK at 5% of what the same
model and provider would cost on it; Cline is BYOK-or-credits.

**Two sentences of `CONTEXT.md` § Models & Money fail at once under it.** Session **Cost** is *"token
cost plus machine cost, blended into one figure; a session never presents the two separately"* — but
the two halves are now billed by different parties, and a blend asserts one invoice holds both.
**Total spend** is *"session Cost plus Seat cost … the only figure that represents what the
Organization actually pays"* — and it stops being that figure the moment the largest component of
agent spend leaves the platform's invoice. What survives is a number that is mostly seats, under a
label promising the whole bill.

The size of the distortion is knowable from the committed fixture, because R-D4 is a ratio with
session spend in its denominator: seat cost is ~$4,212 against ~$4,500 of session spend, **46.2% of
Total spend**, and it is the sharpest finding the product makes. Take token cost out of that
denominator and the finding moves — not because the Organization changed how it works, but because
of a procurement arrangement. A headline that swings on who holds the API key has to say which money
it counts.

**There is a shipped precedent for the shape.** OpenRouter's per-generation record carries an
`is_byok` boolean, and its activity endpoint reports `usage` and **`byok_usage_inference` as two
separate measures** rather than one total — at `date × model × endpoint` grain. It does not blend
them, and neither should we. What it *does* do is estimate the BYOK side: BYOK spend is *"estimated
based on market rates for that provider, and don't reflect any discounts you might have from them"*.
That estimate is the one part of its behaviour this project cannot copy, for the reason ADR-0005
exists.

## Decision

**Deferred.** BYOK is not built and the schema does not anticipate it. The shape is fixed here so it
is not re-derived under pressure later, and so nothing shipped meanwhile forecloses it.

**1. `billing_owner: "platform" | "customer_key"` sits on TokenUsage, not on AgentSession.** A key is
held per vendor; a session routes across vendors, and `TokenUsage` is already stored per
`(AgentSession × Model)` precisely because *"a single AgentSession may consume tokens across more
than one Model"*. A customer holding an Anthropic key and no OpenAI key runs sessions whose money is
**mixed**, and session grain cannot express that.

**2. Cost stays attributed upstream, and is attributed twice.** ADR-0005 stands: billing prices,
analytics aggregates, this application prices nothing. Billing hands over two figures per session —
platform-billed cost and vendor-billed token cost — and `CostBearing` in
`src/domain/metrics/spend.ts` grows a second field. Its stated invariant, *"the only thing done to it
is addition"*, survives; what it loses is that there was only ever one thing to add.

**3. Total spend renders two stacks — "billed by us" and "billed by your vendor".** The stack is
legitimate for the reason § 11 C11 refuses one elsewhere: `billing_owner` **partitions** the money
exactly, where WorkType over a Task-grain measure does not. "Billed by us" holds machine cost,
platform-key token cost and seat cost; "billed by your vendor" holds customer-key token cost. Every
dollar sits in one column, once.

**4. Cost per completed Task keeps both stacks in its numerator.** It is a claim about what getting
work done costs, not about who invoiced it. A numerator restricted to platform-billed money would
read a migration to BYOK as a step change in engineering efficiency — the most misleading thing this
dashboard could print.

**5. The vendor-billed figure comes from the vendor, or it is absent.** It is never priced from this
project's token rate card. R-V8 gives "estimated" to Projected cost alone, and an estimated figure
inside Total spend collapses two labels that warn about two different things. Where a vendor export
cannot be obtained, or cannot be joined to a session, the figure is an **absence** in R-M18/R-V10's
existing sense — a gap and a reason, never a zero and never a guess.

### The hidden-session clause

`CONTEXT.md` defines a **Hidden session** as one that terminated through platform or infrastructure
failure: *"The platform absorbs its cost; it is not billed to the Organization, and it appears in no
metric and no view."*

**Under BYOK the first clause is not something the platform is able to do.** The tokens were bought
with the customer's key and charged by the vendor at the moment of spend; no platform-side write-off
retracts a charge the platform never made. Absorption is only available over money the platform
billed. So the definition acquires a second clause, and it has to read:

> The platform absorbs the cost it billed — machine time, and token cost only where `billing_owner`
> is `platform`. A customer-key session's token cost **cannot** be absorbed, and is therefore not
> silently dropped either: it appears in the vendor-billed total, carrying no session, no Member, no
> Repository and no Task. The exclusion narrows from *"no metric and no view"* to **no
> platform-billed metric, and no session-grain view**.

**This is what makes the retrofit more than a column addition.** R-M2 is a data-layer invariant
applied exactly once — `stored.filter((row) => !row.hidden)` on one line of `src/data/load.ts` — and
ADR-0008 deliberately put the child roll-up on that same line, because *"a per-query fold is a fold
somebody eventually forgets"*. Everything above the data layer is handed one population and is not
offered the alternative. BYOK makes **the visible population and the billed population two different
populations**, and money now has to cross a boundary that sessions must not. Two placements exist:

- `load.ts` returns a second, **session-less** vendor-billed total beside `sessions` and
  `childSessions`, which the spend query alone reads. The single fold survives, and every aggregate
  that ignores the total is silently right.
- Hidden rows survive into the domain layer behind a flag every aggregate must respect. This is the
  per-query fold ADR-0008 refused, and the first aggregate that forgets it publishes a hidden
  session's Member.

**The first is cheaper and is the one recorded here.** Its cost is that the fixture's hidden rows —
15 of the 1,049 on disk, ~1.4% — become load-bearing where today they are discarded, and an
invariant has to pin that total. ADR-0008's own clause extends with it: *a hidden root takes its
children with it*, so removing a hidden root means summing its tree's vendor-billed cost **before**
discarding the tree, not after.

## Why it is deferred

**The decision needs a fact this project cannot yet obtain: what the vendor bill says.** ADR-0005 is
the architecture of every money figure here — authoritative money lives in billing, analytics reads
it. Under BYOK the authoritative source is an invoice the platform never receives, so honouring
ADR-0005 means ingesting the customer's vendor usage export. That is an ingest problem, ticket 55
owns the sketch, and it may not resolve in this decision's favour: OpenRouter's own BYOK measure
arrives at `date × model` grain, which joins to no session, and if a vendor bill is obtainable only
per org-month then per-session `billing_owner` buys nothing a monthly org-level figure does not.
**Fixing the display before the source is known fixes a shape against data that may never arrive in
it.** Every surveyed vendor showing BYOK money estimates it, because none of them holds the bill
either; shipping our estimate would reverse ADR-0005 and spend R-V8's label on a figure already
known to be wrong by whatever discount the customer negotiated.

**It would move the product's sharpest finding for a reason that is not a finding.** Restating R-D4
means new generator targets, a new band in `src/fixtures/invariants.mts`, and a fixture whose
seat-share headline depends on the token/machine split the generator picks rather than on the low
volume that makes the finding true. A demonstration dataset should not carry two billing modes while
the finding it exists to show is being read off it.

**Nothing is blocked by the absence, and one thing is protected by it.** The committed fixture is one
Organization on the platform's own keys — the single-tenant demonstration `spec.md` § 1.1 already
scopes. **The cost of waiting is one field**: adding `billing_owner` to a hand-written validator over
regenerated fixture files is a day's work at any point. **The cost of guessing now is a display
contract, a stacked chart, a label rule and a hidden-session invariant written against an ingest
path that does not exist** — and a half-built BYOK is worse than none, because a two-stack Total
spend on a platform-key Organization renders an empty second column asserting a distinction the data
cannot make. The cheaper mistake to fix later is the missing column.

## What it costs to retrofit

Named concretely, because "some schema work" is what makes a deferral look cheaper than it is.

- **Schema and data.** `TokenUsage` in `src/domain/types.ts` is
  `{ model_id: string } & Record<TokenClass, number>`; a third member breaks that `Record` form.
  `tokenUsageSchema` in `src/data/schema.ts` requires every declared field and treats a missing one
  as a `FixtureFault`, so **all 25 files under `src/fixtures/data/sessions/` are regenerated, not
  patched**. `AgentSession.cost` either gains a sibling or becomes a pair, which edits R-M4's
  one-money-field reading. `src/data/load.ts` gains the session-less vendor total, on the line that
  today holds the hidden strip and the child roll-up.
- **Domain.** `CostBearing` and `totalSpend` in `src/domain/metrics/spend.ts` (two fields, and every
  caller states which it sums); `src/domain/aggregate.ts` wherever money rolls to Member, Team and
  Organization; `src/domain/metrics/projection.ts`, which must say which spend it forecasts.
- **Surfaces.** `/demo/spend`'s Total spend panel — `src/data/queries/spend.ts` carries a two-group
  `SEAT_GROUPS` stack and a ViewModel with `seatCost`, `seatMonths` and `seatShare`, all of which
  acquire a *share of what* question — plus `spend-panels.tsx`; the summary tile and its change
  figure (`src/data/queries/summary.ts`, `summary-tiles.tsx`); `/demo/people`'s ranking, since
  R-M15 sorts by *total* cost and that now has two meanings; `member-profile.tsx`;
  `projection-panel.tsx`; `/demo/history`'s expanded row, the only surface carrying per-session Model
  mix and so the natural home for a per-model billing owner; and `rate-card.tsx`, *"the evidence for
  every money figure in the product"*, which would be evidence for money the platform did not charge.
- **Fixture.** `pricing.mts` prices two owners; `spend.mts` and `targets.mts` size the seat share;
  `invariants.mts` and `invariants.test.ts` gain a band for the split.
- **Tests.** `src/domain/metrics/spend.test.ts` (T-U12/T-U13), `spend.fixture.test.ts`,
  `aggregate.fixture.test.ts`, `load.test.ts`, `spend-panels.test.tsx`, `summary-tiles.test.tsx`,
  `projection-panel.test.tsx`, and **`money-labelling.test.tsx` most of all** — T-C9 asserts across
  the whole of `/demo/spend` that no attributed money figure carries an "estimated" marker, which is
  exactly what a priced BYOK figure would fail. In `e2e/`: `summary.spec.ts`, `ranked.spec.ts`,
  `secondary.spec.ts`, `payload.spec.ts`. And § 11's part-to-whole check — session cost and seat cost
  per Member summing exactly to Total spend per Member — becomes a three-part sum.

**The irreducible part is none of that.** It is the hidden-session clause, R-D4's restatement and
R-V8's label: three positions argued elsewhere in the repository, each of which has to be re-argued
rather than edited.

## Alternatives

**Build it now.** Rejected on the deferral argument: the display would be designed before the data
source, and the fixture would carry two billing modes through its own grading.

**Price the customer's tokens from our rate card and blend one Total spend.** The OpenRouter shape,
and the tempting one — no surface changes at all. Rejected because it makes analytics compute money,
the exact contradiction ADR-0005 was written to remove, and the figure is wrong by a negotiated
discount we cannot see. It also puts "estimated" inside Total spend, collapsing R-V8's two labels
into one that warns about nothing in particular.

**Model BYOK as a rate of zero.** No schema change; customer-key sessions simply cost less. Rejected
— it reads a transfer as a discount. Cost per completed Task would fall by the token share and
tokens would rise while cost stood still, which is precisely the volume/cost divergence
`CONTEXT.md` tells readers to distrust. Zero is also indistinguishable from an absorbed session.

**`billing_owner` on AgentSession.** Cheaper: one column, no per-model split, no change to
`TokenUsage`'s shape. Rejected because a mixed session exists and session grain makes it
unrepresentable. Recorded because it is the change someone will propose on cost grounds, and the
fixture would not falsify it.

**Show vendor-billed money in a panel of its own, beside Total spend rather than inside it.**
Cheapest of the live options: no partition, no stack, no change to Total spend's definition.
Rejected as the primary shape because `CONTEXT.md` makes Total spend *the only figure that represents
what the Organization actually pays*, and two figures side by side mean neither one is — the reader
adds them by hand. **It survives as the fallback** if ticket 55 finds the vendor bill obtainable only
at org-month grain, since a figure that cannot be attributed to a session cannot join a
session-grain stack.

**Keep the Hidden rule as it is and accept the reconciliation gap.** Rejected. Hidden sessions are
~1.4% of rows, so the gap is small — and it is a gap between this dashboard and an invoice the
customer can open in another tab. The size is what makes it worse rather than better: too small to
notice, too specific to explain.

## Consequences

If accepted, the following are amended rather than discovered:

- **`CONTEXT.md` § Models & Money** — Cost (no longer one blended figure), Total spend (two owners),
  Rate card (the token card prices money the platform may not have charged); **§ Work** — Hidden
  session gains the clause above.
- **`spec.md`** — R-M2 (the exclusion narrows), R-M4 (two attributed figures), R-M5 and R-N9 (Total
  spend's composition and its panel), R-V8 (the vendor figure is absent, never estimated), R-M15
  (what "total cost" sorts by). A17 and A25 restate, and the partition needs an acceptance criterion
  of its own.
- **ADR-0005 is extended, not reversed.** The application still prices nothing; it reads two
  attributed figures instead of one. If the vendor total ever has to be *derived* here, that is a
  reversal of ADR-0005 and needs its own ADR.
- **ADR-0008's hidden clause gains a sentence** — a hidden root's tree is summed before it is
  discarded.
- **The product gains a money figure it cannot verify.** Everything on these surfaces today
  reconciles to one invoice; the vendor-billed stack reconciles to a bill the platform does not
  hold, and the honesty of the page then rests on an ingest path.
- **Until this is accepted, the codebase must not anticipate it.** No `billing_owner` in the schema,
  no third series in the Total spend stack, no "billed by" language in copy.

## What would move this to Accepted

Three things, in order: a customer on their own key; ticket 55's ingest sketch resolving whether a
vendor usage export is obtainable at a grain that joins to a session; and a decision on whether Cost
per completed Task spans billing owners — which this ADR proposes it does, and which is the one
position here a customer's finance function is likely to argue with.
